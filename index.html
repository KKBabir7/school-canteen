About SchoolFood

Built for the school lunch rush

SchoolFood is a validation prototype for digital canteen pre-ordering. Families order before break, kitchens prepare with clarity, and managers move orders from New to Ready — in English and Arabic.

  • Wallet limits & food controls
  • Bilingual menus for every canteen
  • Multi-role access in a single session

What each side of the school gets

How it works

From kitchen setup to pickup — the same loop schools need to validate.

01

Set canteens and bilingual menus

Providers create locations, sections, and items with EN/AR names, then assign menus to canteens.

02

Families order before the break

Parents or students build a tray within wallet balance, daily limits, and food restrictions.

03

Managers fulfill on the floor

Orders move New → Preparing → Ready → Completed so food is waiting at break time.
