How it works

A simple operational loop designed for schools validating digital canteen ordering.

Set up canteens

Food providers register physical locations and keep contact details current.

Build & assign menus

Create sections and items once, then assign menus to the right canteens.

Fulfill daily orders

Canteen managers move orders from New to Ready with a clear status workflow.
